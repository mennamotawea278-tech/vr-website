What is VR?

Virtual Reality is technology that places you inside a computer-generated 3D environment you can look around and interact with.

360°Immersive view
6DoFFull body movement tracking
90Hz+Smooth refresh rate
3DSpatial audio and visuals

How does it work?

Four simple parts make the magic happen.

Headset displayTwo screens show slightly different images to each eye, creating depth.
Motion trackingSensors follow your head and hands so the view moves with you.
ControllersHandheld devices let you grab, point, and interact with objects.
SoftwareEngines like Unity and Unreal render the virtual world in real time.

Types of VR headsets

Pick the one that fits how and where you want to use VR.

📶

Standalone

Everything is built in. No cable or PC needed. Easy to carry and set up. Example: Meta Quest.

🖥️

PC VR

Connects to a powerful computer for top graphics and detailed simulations.

🕹️

Console VR

Designed for a game console, giving a simple plug-and-play experience. Example: PlayStation VR2.

📱

Mobile VR

Uses a phone inside a simple viewer. Cheap, but with limited features.
